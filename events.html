Community Events & Gatherings

Connecting with families and making a tangible impact, one event at a time.

Beverage Bonanza @ McNichols Park

We had a fantastic turnout at McNichols Park for our Beverage Bonanza! Our team, along with our volunteers, distributed cases of drinks to families in our community. We believe in building trust and positive relationships with the people we serve, one interaction at a time.

Annual Sock Hop

We are proud to announce our annual Sock Hop event in partnership with the NAACP Denver branch! During the month of August, we collected donations to provide socks for those in need in our community. Socks are often one of the most requested but least donated items at shelters, and our goal is to help meet this need. Thank you to everyone who donated and helped make this event a success!
