Building Brighter Futures

Showing our youth what’s possible when they aim high at the University of Colorado Boulder

Empowering Youth Through Higher Education Partnerships

McBride Impact is proud to partner with the University of Colorado, creating unique opportunities for youth in our programs to experience college life firsthand.

Through this partnership, we take young people to sporting events on campus, where they can immerse themselves in the vibrant culture of the university and envision themselves as part of it one day.

These trips go beyond just watching a game—they offer our youth a chance to explore the possibilities of higher education in a setting that many may not have had the chance to see otherwise.

For many, this experience sparks dreams of pursuing a college degree, instilling in them the belief that they too can be part of a college community.

“We are grateful for this opportunity to broaden their horizons & show them what they can achieve!”
