Kids Kreation

Homemade Dessert Entrepreneurship Program

At McBride Impact we believe in empowering youth with entrepreneurial skills through hands-on experience. Our Kids Kreations Program provides an opportunity for youth to facilitate and manage their own business, focusing on the creation of homemade desserts.

Program Highlights & Curriculum

Entrepreneurial Skills

Product development, cost management, branding, and marketing strategies for real-world application.

Homemade Desserts

Participants create their own delicious ice cream flavors while understanding unit production costs and profit margins.

Graphic & Brand Design

Designing marketing collateral, packaging materials, and consumer promotion strategies.
