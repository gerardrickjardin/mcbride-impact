Can you imagine a world where youth violence is cut in half in one year? We can.

We created a blueprint called “Impact for Change” to reduce youth violence by 50% in one year. We combine old school street wisdom with new school social technology. We are building a national network of change makers. Sign up to receive the “Impact for Change” blueprint.

Thank you! Your "Impact for Change" blueprint is on its way to your inbox.

The Three "Impact for Change" Components

Hyper-Engaging Curriculum

Immersive Story-Based Curriculum that teaches goal achievement and problem-solving that keeps students hooked.

Deep Trauma Intervention

Healing trauma at the root level so inner-city kids can actually thrive, not just survive.
