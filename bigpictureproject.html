The Big Picture Project

The World’s Largest Anti-Youth Violence Collaborative Artwork

The project is called The Big Picture. It centers on creating anti-youth violence and anti-school shooting posters from different communities across various states. Each location will contribute its own design, and these individual pieces will eventually be brought together to form one unified, large-scale visual statement. The goal is to create the world’s largest collaborative poster advocating against youth violence and school shootings – symbolizing collective action, awareness, and change.
