Memorial Scholarship Fund

Empowering outstanding students of color through educational equity, mentorship, and academic excellence.

Donate to the Fund

Carrying Forward a Legacy of Leadership

Jason McBride's establishment of The John McBride Memorial Scholarship reflects a deep commitment to preserving his father's legacy while extending opportunities to the next generation.

John McBride was a passionate advocate for youth empowerment, spending much of his life working toward the betterment of his community. His dedication to fostering a brighter future for Denver's youth made him a beloved figure in the city, and his impact was felt across numerous programs aimed at uplifting young people, particularly those facing challenges due to systemic inequities.

The John McBride Memorial Scholarship aims to carry forward his vision by supporting students who exhibit not only academic achievement but also a passion for leadership and giving back to their communities. It seeks to empower students of color, helping them overcome barriers and create a path toward higher education and success.

Applicants who demonstrate excellence in school, leadership in their communities, and a commitment to creating positive change are encouraged to apply for this scholarship to help continue the transformative work John McBride began.

We invite students who excel academically, lead with purpose in their communities, and are committed to driving positive change to apply for this scholarship.

Your application will help advance the meaningful legacy that John McBride established.

The McBride Impact is a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit organization dedicated to youth violence prevention, trauma intervention, and educational empowerment.
